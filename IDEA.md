IndustryX is a server-authoritative idle factory and economic simulation game where players build industries, manage production chains, trade resources, research technologies, and grow a global industrial empire.

The game features interconnected systems including factories, workers, storage, energy, transportation, markets, contracts, automation, weather, events, prestige, and mega projects. Resource production and market prices respond dynamically to player activity, supply, demand, and global events.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, and Vercel, IndustryX uses server-controlled gameplay logic and persistent player data to support fair progression, prevent manipulation, and provide a scalable foundation for future multiplayer and competitive features.
